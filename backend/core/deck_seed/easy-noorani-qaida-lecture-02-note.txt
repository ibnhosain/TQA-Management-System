Lecture 2 · Day 2 — What we learned today

📖 Easy Noorani Qaida
• Duas before learning — the 4th dua, "Open my heart":
  رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِيْ صَدْرِيْ وَيَسِّرْ لِيْ اَمْرِيْ — My Lord, open my heart for me, make my task easy for me,
  وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّنْ لِّسَانِيْ يَفْقَهُوْا قَوْلِيْ — and untie the knot from my tongue, so they can understand what I say.
• Lesson 1 — the second row: ح Haa · خ Kha · د Dal · ذ Dhal · ر Ra
• Add the dots: ح with a dot below is ج Jeem; with a dot above it is خ Kha. د with a dot above is ذ Dhal.
• Read from the right — one finger, one letter.

📖 My First Surahs — Al-Fatihah
• Verse 2: اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعٰلَمِيْنَ — All praise is for Allah, the Lord of all the worlds.

📖 Islamic Duas, Manners and Values — Lesson 1: Eating and Drinking
• If I forget to say Bismillah: بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ فِيْ اَوَّلِهٖ وَاٰخِرِهٖ — "In the name of Allah, at its beginning and its end." (Jami' at-Tirmidhi)
• After eating: اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِيْ اَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا وَجَعَلَنَا مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِيْنَ — "All praise is for Allah who gave us food and drink and made us Muslims." (Abu Dawud and at-Tirmidhi)
• I say Alhamdulillah when I finish.

📖 My Salah Duas — Dua 2: Thana (first part)
• سُبْحٰنَكَ اللّٰهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعَالٰى جَدُّكَ — "Glory be to You, O Allah, and all praise is Yours. Blessed is Your name, high is Your majesty …"
• We say it after the first Takbir, standing with hands folded, in the first rak'ah only — once, quietly. (Sunnah)

✏️ My First Arabic Writing Book — Warm-up
• Tall lines: start at the top and pull down (like Alif).
• Little boats: start on the right (like Ba).
• Deep cups: start on the right (like Noon).

🏠 Homework (5–10 minutes)
1. After dinner, say the dua for after eating.
2. Say verses 1 and 2 of Al-Fatihah to Ammu or Abbu.
3. Point to Haa, Kha, Dal, Dhal, Ra in your book and say their names.
4. Trace tall lines, boats and cups with your finger.

For parents: ask your child "Which one is Kha?" and let them point to it.
